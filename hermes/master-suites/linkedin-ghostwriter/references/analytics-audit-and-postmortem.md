# LinkedIn Content Analytics Audit & Post-Mortem Diagnostic

The only objective benchmark of what works on your account is your own historical post data. Rather than guessing why a post underperformed, use this forensic diagnostic methodology.

---

## 1. Exporting Raw LinkedIn Data

1. Navigate to: **LinkedIn $\rightarrow$ Analytics $\rightarrow$ Content $\rightarrow$ Export**.
2. Download the CSV export containing impressions, unique views, clicks, reactions, comments, and reposts.
3. Sort your last 30 to 50 publications by **Engagement Rate (%)**:
   $$\text{Engagement Rate} = \frac{\text{Reactions} + \text{Comments} + \text{Reposts}}{\text{Impressions}} \times 100$$

---

## 2. The 3 Diagnostic Quadrants

```
                    High Impressions
                           │
       Quadrant 2:         │        Quadrant 1:
    ALGORITHMIC REACH      │     THE OUTLIER WINNER
    (Broad curiosity,      │    (High reach, high
     low buyer intent)     │     ICP conversation)
                           │
───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────
                           │
       Quadrant 4:         │        Quadrant 3:
      THE TOTAL FLOP       │     THE NICHE GEM
    (Hook failed,          │    (Low impressions,
     zero distribution)    │     ultra-qualified DMs)
                           │
                    Low Impressions
                           
       Low Engagement ─────────────── High Engagement
```

### Quadrant 1: The Outlier Winner (Top 10%)
- **Signals:** Engagement rate $> 4.5\%$, comments $> 25$, comments from target ICP titles.
- **Action:** This is a proven theme. Recyle into a Carousel (Mode 4), a Twitter thread (Mode 10), and an expanded newsletter piece.

### Quadrant 2: The Broad Reach Trap
- **Signals:** 20,000+ impressions, but comments are 1-word generic praises (*"Congrats!"*, *"So true!"*). Zero inbound DMs.
- **Diagnosis:** The hook was too broad or dramatic. It attracted consumer curiosity rather than B2B buyers.
- **Action:** Tighten the specificity. Add hard technical constraints to repel tourists and attract decision-makers.

### Quadrant 3: The Niche Gem
- **Signals:** Only 1,200 impressions, but generated 3 high-intent inbound DMs or qualified leads.
- **Diagnosis:** Algorithmic reach was constrained because the topic was deep technical architecture, but buyer resonance was 100%.
- **Action:** Keep publishing these. Revenue comes from Quadrant 3, not Quadrant 2.

### Quadrant 4: The Flop Post-Mortem
When a post receives $< 500$ impressions and $< 3$ comments:
1. **Did the Hook Fail?** Did the first line fail to create tension before char 140 on mobile?
2. **Did you include an Outbound Link in the body?** If yes, LinkedIn algorithm cut reach by 50%.
3. **Did you post and leave immediately?** If you did not reply to early comments during the first 60 minutes, momentum died.
4. **Is it AI Slop?** If the post contained generic phrases (*"delve"*, *"crucial"*, *"mindset"*), readers scrolled past without clicking `...see more`.
