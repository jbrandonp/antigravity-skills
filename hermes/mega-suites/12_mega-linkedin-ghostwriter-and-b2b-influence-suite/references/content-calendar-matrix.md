# Weekly Content Calendar & Strategic Matrix

A sustainable weekly rhythm balancing authority, technical depth, contrarian takes, and founder vulnerability to maintain compound LinkedIn audience growth without creator burnout.

---

## 1. The 4-Bucket Content Allocation

A high-performing B2B LinkedIn profile rotates through 4 distinct content buckets:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  40% AUTHORITY & PROOF (Teardowns, metrics, wins)      │
├──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┤
│  25% TACTICAL / HOW-TO   │  20% CONTRARIAN TAKE        │
│  (Frameworks, cheatsheets│  (Debunking bad practices,  │
│   code, devlogs)         │   daring market perspectives│
├──────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┤
│  15% FOUNDER VULNERABILITY (Mistakes, lessons, journey)│
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. The Pre-Post Engagement Ritual (The 15/30 Rule)

Posting into a vacuum kills organic reach. Execute the 15/30 routine:
- **15 Minutes BEFORE Posting:** Open LinkedIn and leave 3 to 5 thoughtful, value-adding comments on posts written by leaders in your target audience list. This signals to LinkedIn's graph engine that your session is actively interactive.
- **Publish Post:** Drop the publication at the scheduled time.
- **30 Minutes AFTER Posting (The Golden Hour):** Stay active. Reply to every single incoming comment within 5 to 10 minutes to trigger the algorithm's second-wave distribution loop.
