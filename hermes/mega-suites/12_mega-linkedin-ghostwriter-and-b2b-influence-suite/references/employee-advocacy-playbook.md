# LinkedIn Employee Advocacy & Team Amplification Playbook

Employee posts on LinkedIn generate on average **8x more engagement and 561% more reach** than posts published by the corporate brand page. However, forced or robotic advocacy ("Please share this company press release") triggers internal resentment and zero reach.

---

## 1. The 3 Tiers of Team Amplification

Never mandate exact copy-paste sharing. Standardize these 3 authentic tiers:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ TIER 1: QUOTE REPOST WITH OPERATIONAL PERSPECTIVE      │
│ The employee reposts the founder/company post, adding  │
│ 2-3 sentences of their own frontline experience.       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER 2: THOUGHT-PARTNER COMMENT                        │
│ The employee leaves an insightful, detailed comment    │
│ expanding on one specific bullet in the post.          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ TIER 3: PERSONAL DEEP-DIVE POST                        │
│ The employee authors their own post detailing how they │
│ solved a specific technical challenge for the project. │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## 2. The 14-Day Advocacy Launch Framework

| Phase | Days | Focus & Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Foundation** | Days 1–3 | **Profile Polish:** Run the 100-point profile audit on the 5–10 core team members. Align banners, headline value propositions, and role descriptions. |
| **Phase 2: Story Mining** | Days 4–7 | **Voice & Content Bank:** Conduct 15-minute interviews with team leads to extract 3 concrete wins, 2 bugs solved, and 1 client insight. |
| **Phase 3: The Pilot Wave** | Days 8–10 | **Staggered Publishing:** Schedule 2 employee posts per week on alternating days. Team members comment on each other's posts within the Golden Hour. |
| **Phase 4: Optimization** | Days 11–14 | **ROI Measurement:** Review reach, profile views, inbound candidate inquiries, and inbound prospect leads. Double down on top-performing topics. |

---

## 3. Brand Guardrails (Zero Cringe, 100% Protection)

- **Allowed & Encouraged:**
  - Sharing technical architectures, bug post-mortems, and engineering trade-offs.
  - Personal lessons learned during product sprints.
  - Screenshots of clean terminal outputs, architecture diagrams, or open-source releases.
- **Strictly Prohibited:**
  - Revealing unreleased client names without written consent.
  - Sharing raw financial margins or unredacted contract values.
  - Generic corporate cheerleading (*"Proud to work for the greatest company on earth!"*).
