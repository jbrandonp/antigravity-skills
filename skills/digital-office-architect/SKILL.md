An exhaustive, battle-tested operational framework and engineering methodology for designing, selling, deploying, and maintaining digital administrative organization, pipeline tracking, and lightweight automation services for **ANY small business (TPE / PME from 1 to 10 employees)**, regardless of industry.

## 🧭 PHILOSOPHY : THE "ANTI-SAAS, PRO-ENTREPRENEUR" BLUEPRINT

Whether an entrepreneur installs heat pumps, develops web software, repairs car gearboxes, treats sports injuries, or bakes bread for restaurants, **95 % of their administrative failure points are identical** :

1. **The Upstream Revenue Leak :** Small business owners are immersed in technical delivery. They issue a price, quote, or proposal, and then forget to follow up. Without a structured 7-to-14-day reminder system, **30 % to 45 % of deals are lost** to faster competitors or forgotten by clients.
2. **The "Software Bloat" Trap :** Heavy SaaS ERPs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Vertuoza, Bouwsoft, Odoo) demand 100–300 €/month plus heavy onboarding fees and require full-time administrative staff to fill 25 fields per contact. In a 1-to-10 person business, these tools are abandoned within 90 days in favor of glove-compartment receipts and WhatsApp notes.
3. **The Missing Weekly Ritual :** Without a fixed, 15-minute weekly habit (Friday at 16h30), receipts, unpaid invoices, and overdue quotes accumulate until the accountant sounds the alarm at quarter-end.
4. **Mandatory Peppol 2026 E-Invoicing :** The European directive and Belgian legislation enforce structured XML UBL e-invoicing for **ALL B2B companies**, across all sectors.
5. **Absent Legal Shield :** Work begun on verbal agreements or SMS exchanges, zero liability caps, zero deposits, and unchecked scope creep.

The **Bureau Numérique Universel** solves this with a **universal 4-pillar architectural skeleton** combined with a **sector-specific adaptation matrix**.

### PILIER 1 : SOCIO-FISCAL & REGULATORY SHIELD (BELGIUM 2026 / EUROPE)

When operating or advising under Belgian regulations, enforce these strict legal parameters (detailed in `references/socio_fiscal_belgium_matrix.md`):

#### 1.1 The Smart Coop Salaried Framework
* **Management Contribution :** 6.5 % deducted from every invoice HTVA.
* **Mutualized Solidarity Funds :** ~8 % on the employment budget (accident insurance, wage guarantee).
* **Net Wage Transformation Wedge :**
  * *Student Contract (Contingent 600h) :* ONSS solidarity of 2.71 % (worker) + 5.43 % (employer). Net return: **~70 % to 75 %** of invoice HTVA.
  * *Standard Employee Contract (CP 200) :* ONSS patronale (~25 %), ONSS personnelle (13.07 %), and progressive withholding tax. Net return: **~45 % to 50 %** of invoice HTVA.
* **Minimum Invoicing Floor :** Never issue an invoice below **250,00 € HTVA** to cover CP 200 minimum daily wage requirements (RMMMG).
* **Dimona Pre-Declaration :** Every work mission must be declared via Smart **before midnight prior to the day of work**. Retroactive declarations are strictly illegal.
* **Software Resale Ban :** Smart strictly forbids reselling software licenses (M365, Google). The client must subscribe directly.

#### 1.2 Student-Indépendant (INASTI 2026) vs Smart Equilibrium
* **Exemption Threshold 2026 :** **0 € social contributions** if Net Taxable Income < **8 687,04 € / year**.
* **Equilibrium Formula :**
  $$\text{Smart Cost} \approx 14.5\ \% \times CA \quad \text{vs} \quad \text{Accountant Cost} \approx 900\ €$$
  $$0.145 \times CA = 900 \implies CA \approx 6\ 200\ €\text{ HTVA}$$
* **Decision Rule :** Below 6 000 €/year $\rightarrow$ Remain in Smart. Above 6 500 €/year $\rightarrow$ Student-Independent with certified accountant.

#### 1.3 Personal Income Tax, Family Benefits & VAT Matrix
* **Parent Tax Cliff ("Enfant à charge") :** Maximum net student resources ~3 980 € to 4 500 € net/year to protect parents' tax deduction (~1 500 € to 2 200 € extra tax risk).
* **Family Benefits (FAMIWAL) :** Max **240 hours/quarter** under standard contracts.
* **Universal VAT Matrix (Belgium) :**
  * *Services, Consulting, Maintenance, Digital :* **21 % standard rate**.
  * *Construction & Renovation (BTP) :* **6 %** (private residential > 10 years) or **21 %** (new build / commercial).
  * *Healthcare & Therapeutic Care :* **0 % exempt** (Code TVA art. 44). Esthetic care: **21 %**.
  * *Food & Beverage (Commerce B2B) :* **6 %** (raw food) / **12 %** (catering without alcohol) / **21 %** (alcohol & equipment).

#### 1.4 Peppol 2026 B2B E-Invoicing Demarcation
* European/Belgian Peppol mandate (effective Jan 1, 2026) mandates structured XML UBL format **for ALL B2B invoices**, NOT for quotes or internal tracking sheets.
* Commercial pitch: *"40 % of quotes never become Peppol invoices because they are forgotten in the field."*

### PILIER 2 : ANTI-SAAS PROTECTED SPREADSHEET COCKPIT

The core deliverable is an automated Excel tracking system built on ubiquitous software (Excel / Google Sheets) with zero new subscription overhead.

#### 2.1 The 3-Sheet Universal Blueprint
1. **Sheet 1: `Suivi des Affaires` (or `Suivi des Devis`)** :
   * Columns: `N° Affaire/Devis`, `Date Émission`, `Nom du Client`, `Téléphone/Contact`, `Objet de la Prestation`, `Montant HTVA (€)`, `Statut Actuel`, `Date Relance Prévue`, `Statut Relance (Auto)`, `Dernier Contact`, `Commentaires/Action`.
2. **Sheet 2: `Tableau de Bord` (Executive Dashboard)** :
   * KPI Cards: Total pending deals, Pending revenue (€), Signed won revenue (€), Urgent overdue deals count.
   * Dynamic formulas: `=COUNTIF(...)`, `=SUMIF(...)`, conditional color alerts.
3. **Sheet 3: `Rituel Hebdomadaire` (15-Minute Friday Checklist)** :
   * 4 chronological steps: Open file (2m), Turn won deals green (3m), Check red overdue lines (5m), Make 3 calls (5m).

#### 2.2 Security & Ergonomics Rules
* **Password Protection :** Protect sheets with standard password (`OFFICE2026` or `BTP2026`) to prevent broken formulas.
* **Input Column Unlocking :** Columns 1 through 8, 10, and 11 MUST have `cell.protection = Protection(locked=False)`.
* **Formula Locking :** Only Column 9 (`Statut Relance`) and headers remain locked (`Protection(locked=True)`).
* **Live Formulas Injected in Empty Rows (Rows 5 to 100) :**
  * *Column 8 (Target follow-up date) :* `=IF(B12="","",B12 + DELAY_DAYS)` (where `DELAY_DAYS` is sector-parameterized).
  * *Column 9 (Automated status alert) :*  
    `=IF(G12="","",IF(G12="Accepté","Gagné",IF(G12="Refusé","Clôturé",IF(OR(G12="En attente",G12="Envoyé"),IF(AND(H12<>"",TODAY()>H12),"À RELANCER D'URGENCE","Dans les temps"),IF(G12="À préparer","En cours bureau","")))))`
* **Disclaimer Isolation :** Data parsing and CSV exports must filter footer disclaimers (`num_str.startswith("AVERTISSEMENT")`) to guarantee zero phantom rows.

## 📋 SECTION 3 : BULLETPROOF 1-PAGE CONTRACTING & 9 CGV CLAUSES

Every deployment is governed by a 1-page order form (`BON_DE_COMMANDE_PROPOSITION_COMMERCIALE.md`) with 9 universal protective clauses (detailed in `references/contracts_and_cgv_handbook.md`):

1. **Obligation of Means :** Provider delivers organization and configuration; never liable for client closing rates.
2. **Liability Cap :** Total liability capped strictly at the net amount actually received for the mission.
3. **Scope Creep Firewall :** Initial pack includes a maximum of **50 imported historic deals/contacts**. Additional imports billed per batch.
4. **14-Day Adjustment Warranty :** Free formula adjustments and configuration fixes for 14 calendar days.
5. **Asynchronous Support SLA :** Support via WhatsApp voice/text and email answered within 24h during dedicated windows (7h30-8h30 / 17h00-18h30). Zero live telephone interruptions during business hours.
6. **RGPD Compliance & Zero Local Retention :** Client is Data Controller; provider is Technical Processor without local storage of client databases.
7. **50/50 Payment Terms :** 50% deposit on signing; 50% balance upon delivery and protection password handover.
8. **Third-Party Independence :** Client pays their M365/Google Workspace subscriptions directly.
9. **Intellectual Property Protection :** Client receives perpetual usage rights on the configured sheet; scripts and underlying methodologies remain provider's IP.
