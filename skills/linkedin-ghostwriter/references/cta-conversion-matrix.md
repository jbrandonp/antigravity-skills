# LinkedIn Call-to-Action (CTA) & Conversion Matrix

The end of a LinkedIn post is where attention converts into inbound pipeline, relationship capital, or comment velocity. Using a generic *"Thoughts?"* or *"Subscribe to my newsletter"* converts at the noise floor.

---

## 1. The 6 Tiers of Conversion CTAs

Select the CTA tier matching the post's objective:

| Tier | Objective | Friction | Algorithm Impact | Exact Formula & Example |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Open Debate / Conversation** | Ultra-Low | 🚀 Max Reach (High Comment Velocity) | *"Where do you stand: do you prefer strict type definitions or runtime inference? Why?"* |
| **Tier 2** | **Resource Magnet / Lead Bait** | Low | 🚀 Viral Lift (Comment Loops) | *"I put the full SQLite indexing checklist into a clean 1-page markdown doc. Drop 'SQLITE' in the comments and I'll send it over."* |
| **Tier 3** | **Inbound DM Bridge** | Medium | 💼 High Intent (Direct Pipeline) | *"If you're currently auditing your scraping pipeline for anti-bot blocks, send me a DM with your current proxy setup—happy to share our unblock rate benchmarks."* |
| **Tier 4** | **Profile Funnel** | Low | 👤 Profile Views & Follower Growth | *"We document our entire open-source architecture in public. Check the featured link on my profile to read the full technical spec."* |
| **Tier 5** | **1st Comment Outbound Link** | Low | 🛡️ Preserves Reach (Zero Penalty) | *"Dropping the open-source GitHub repository link in the first comment below 👇"* |
| **Tier 6** | **High-Intent Consultation / Deal** | High | 💰 Pipeline (Use Max 1x/Month) | *"We have bandwidth to onboard 2 engineering teams for our AI reliability audit in Q1. If your team is shipping LLMs to production, link to apply in 1st comment."* |

---

## 2. The Strict Outbound Link Rule

> [!CRITICAL]
> **Never place external outbound URLs in the main body of the post.**  
> LinkedIn penalizes outbound links in the body with a **40% to 60% reach suppression penalty** because it drives users off-platform.
> Always place links in the **1st comment**, or route readers to the **Featured Link on your profile**.

---

## 3. The Comment Lead Magnet Execution Cycle

When using **Tier 2 (Resource Magnet)**:
1. State the exact resource, its format (.md, .pdf, .json), and what it solves.
2. Specify the trigger word in capital letters (e.g., `COMMENT 'DEEP'`).
3. During the **Golden Hour** (first 60 minutes), reply to each comment manually:
   - *"Sent to your DMs, [Name]! Let me know if the connection pooling section is clear."*
   - Delivering the resource directly in the DM opens a 1-to-1 conversation channel with a warm prospect.
